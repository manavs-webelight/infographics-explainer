# Scene catalog (generated from schema/scene-schema.ts)

A scene uses EXACTLY ONE of: one scene-level "narration" (12-60 words)
or "steps" (each step carries its own narration, 6-40 words each).
flow/bullets are capped at 5 steps (items on screen); other types 2-8.
